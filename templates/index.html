⚠️ Model Assumptions & Limitations (Simulated Environment)

  • No real traffic signals are physically altered or controlled.

📐 Transparent ETA Formula

ETA_seconds = (remaining_distance_m / effective_speed_mps) + expected_signal_delay_s
effective_speed_mps = (base_speed_kmh / 3.6) × (1 − congestion_factor)
expected_signal_delay_s = remaining_signalised_junctions × signal_delay_per_junction_s

Label: Rule-based deterministic model. Congestion factors are mapped per segment risk level (Low: 0.1, Med: 0.3, High: 0.5).
